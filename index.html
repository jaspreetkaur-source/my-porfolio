Hi, I’m Jaspreet Kaur — a UI/UX designer who enjoys shaping and design digital experiences that feel intuitive, thoughtful, and easy to navigate.

I’m interested in the small details that make a product feel effortless: My goal and focus is to make products clear, accessible, interactive and easy to use. A clear hierarchy, a helpful interaction, or a journey that simply makes sense.
